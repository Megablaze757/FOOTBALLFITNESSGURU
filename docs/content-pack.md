# PocketAthlete — pre-launch content pack

Everything here is grounded in what the app actually does. Nothing claims user
numbers, results or endorsements you don't have — those are the claims that get
a young brand torn apart in the replies, and they're the ones you can't walk
back.

Every link should carry your referral code so the admin panel attributes it:
`pocketathlete.com/waitlist?ref=YOURCODE`

---

## 1. The one line

> **Your program doesn't know you played 90 minutes yesterday. This one does.**

Alternates, depending on the audience:

- For players: *"A centre back and a winger shouldn't train the same. Yours does."*
- For parents: *"A private performance coach is £100+ an hour. This is £15 a month."*
- For coaches: *"Build one program, assign it to your whole squad, see who actually did it."*

## 2. Content pillars

Rotate these. Four pillars stops the feed becoming one long advert.

| Pillar | What it does | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| **Free coaching** | A real drill, fully explained. Useful even if they never sign up. | 5 in 10 |
| **The problem** | Name the thing generic programs get wrong. | 2 in 10 |
| **Build in public** | What you shipped this week, what broke, what's next. | 2 in 10 |
| **The ask** | Join the waitlist. Direct, once. | 1 in 10 |

The 5:2:2:1 matters. An account that only sells gets no reach; an account that
only teaches gets no signups.

## 4. Short-form video scripts

### Script 1 — "The wall drill" (20–25s)

| Beat | Shot | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | You, ball, wall. Already passing. | "Twelve football drills that need nobody but you." |
| 3–8s | Close on feet, wall passing | "Wall passing. Five metres back. Take the return with your **far** foot." |
| 8–14s | Same drill, slow-mo of the touch | "Not this foot. That one. Away from where a defender would be." |
| 14–20s | You, to camera | "Your first touch decides the pass. Set the ball outside your body — not under it." |
| 20–25s | Text card | "All 38 free. Link in bio." |

*Hook rule: the drill starts before the sentence does. Nobody waits through an
intro.*

| Beat | Shot | Words |
|---|---|---|

| 7–11s | Winger beating a full back | "He needs to beat a man at pace." |
| 11–15s | To camera | "So why is the program the same? Position guides, free — link in bio." |

### Script 3 — "It reads the note" (20s)

| Beat | Shot | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Screen recording, typing into the notes box | "I typed: I don't train legs." |
| 4–10s | Program generating | "Every other app I've used gave me squats on day one." |
| 10–16s | Scroll the program — no leg work, and the constraint listed | "This one moved the volume to upper body and told me it had." |
| 16–20s | To camera | "That's the whole idea. Link in bio." |

*A screen recording of your own product doing the thing is the most persuasive
asset you have. It's proof, not a claim.*

## 5. Launch sequence

Work backwards from the date. Don't announce a date until you've put a real card
through Checkout.

**T-21 — Start posting.** Free coaching only. No mention of launch. You're
building an audience that has a reason to follow you.

**T-14 — Name it.** "I've been building this for X months. It opens on [date]."
Post the build-in-public piece. First waitlist push.

**T-7 — Show it.** Screen recordings. The program generating. The readiness
score changing after a bad night's sleep. The check-in taking 40 seconds.

**T-3 — Founding member framing.** "Waitlist closes when we open. Founding
pricing is locked for as long as you stay subscribed."

**T-0 — Open.** Post the same day you email the waitlist. Reply to everything
for 48 hours.

**T+7 — Proof.** First real user screenshots (with permission). *Now* you can
talk about results, because now you have some.

## 6. Where this actually goes

Ranked by fit for a young sports audience:

1. **TikTok / Reels / Shorts** — the drill videos. Highest ceiling, and the
   solo-drill format is inherently watchable.
2. **Instagram carousels** — the position guides. Saves and shares are the
   signal, and "what a centre back should train" gets saved.
3. **Local club WhatsApp / group chats** — the single highest-converting channel
   you have and the one you'll neglect. Your own club, your teammates' clubs,
   your coach's contacts. One message with a real drill link beats a month of
   posting into the void.
4. **Reddit** — r/bootroom, r/footballtactics, r/running. Only ever post the
   free guide, never the product. These communities will destroy you for an
   advert and reward you for the drill breakdown.
5. **X** — build in public. Small audience, but it's where other builders and
   early adopters are.

## 7. Rules — do not break these

**Never claim:**
- User numbers, revenue or growth you don't have
- Testimonials or results from anyone who hasn't given them to you in writing
- "Trusted by pros / academies / clubs" unless it is literally true
- Anything medical. Not "fixes your injury", not "prevents ACL tears", not
  "diagnoses". The app builds a plan and flags red flags. That's it.

**Always:**
- Say it's general training information, not medical advice, on any
  injury-adjacent post
- Use your `?ref=` link so attribution works
- Reply to every comment for the first hour — it's the only reach lever you
  control for free

**On the under-16 point:** your terms say the app isn't for under-16s. If your
content is landing with 13-year-olds, that gap becomes a real problem fast. Get
the age gate in before you push hard on TikTok.
